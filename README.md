## A.INTRODUCTION
Spiderman

*Spider-Man* is one of the most iconic and beloved superhero franchises in the world, originating from Marvel Comics and created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko. The character first appeared in 1962 and has since been brought to life through blockbuster films, starting with Sam Raimi’s *Spider-Man* trilogy in 2002. The story follows Peter Parker, an ordinary teenager who gains extraordinary abilities after being bitten by a genetically altered spider. Instead of using his powers for personal gain, he chooses to become a hero, learning the timeless lesson that *“with great power comes great responsibility.”* The films blend thrilling action, emotional storytelling, and relatable coming‑of‑age moments that resonate with audiences of all ages.

## B.SYNOPSIS
Peter Parker is a shy, brilliant high school student living in Queens, New York, who is often overlooked by his peers. During a school field trip, he is bitten by a radioactive spider, which grants him amazing powers: superhuman strength, speed, agility, the ability to stick to walls, and a “spider‑sense” that warns him of danger. At first, Peter tries to use his newfound abilities to make money, but a tragic mistake leads to the death of his beloved Uncle Ben. Overcome with grief and guilt, Peter realizes what Uncle Ben always told him is true, those with power must use it to help others. He creates the Spider‑Man identity, builds web‑shooting devices, and dedicates himself to protecting New York City. Along the way, he faces powerful villains such as the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, and many more, all while balancing the challenges of school, friendships, family, and his secret life as a hero.

## C. CHARACTERS
1. **Peter Parker / Spider‑Man** – The main protagonist. He is kind‑hearted, clever, and brave, always striving to do the right thing even when it is difficult.
2. **Uncle Ben** – Peter’s wise and caring guardian. His death deeply shapes Peter and inspires him to become a hero.
3. **Aunt May** – Peter’s warm and loving aunt. She provides him with guidance, support, and a loving home.
4. **Mary Jane Watson** – Peter’s close friend and love interest. She is strong, talented, and one of the few people who sees the real Peter beneath the mask.
5. **Norman Osborn / Green Goblin** – One of Spider‑Man’s greatest enemies. A brilliant scientist driven mad by an experimental serum, he becomes a dangerous threat to the city.

## D. PERSONAL REVIEW

### Why I Chose This Movie

I chose *Spider‑Man* because he is more than just a superhero — he feels like someone we could actually know. Unlike many heroes who are wealthy or born with special gifts, Peter Parker starts as just a regular kid who faces everyday struggles alongside his superhero duties. His journey reminds us that heroism isn’t about being perfect; it’s about doing your best to help others no matter who you are.

### Things I Liked

-**The message of responsibility** — The core theme that our choices matter and that we should look out for one another is powerful and timeless.
-**Relatable hero** — Peter deals with school stress, family worries, and feeling like an outsider, which makes his story feel personal and inspiring.
-**Exciting action and heart** — The movie has incredible stunts and battles, but it also has real emotion and heart that stays with you long after it ends.

### Rating
10 out 10

### Recommendation

I highly recommend *Spider‑Man* to everyone — whether you love superhero movies or not. It is an exciting, heartfelt story about courage, kindness, and doing the right thing. It is suitable for viewers of all ages and will leave you feeling inspired to be your best self.
